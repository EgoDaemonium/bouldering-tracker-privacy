Bouldering Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Bouldering Tracker is an Android app for logging climbing sessions. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: your data stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Everything you enter: sessions, locations, climbs, grades, attempts, notes, photos you attach, and your settings. It is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any server.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Backups and exports

When you choose to save a backup (JSON) or export a spreadsheet (CSV), the file is written to the place you pick. What happens to it afterwards is up to you. Android's own device backup may include the app's data if you have it turned on; that is handled by Google under your Android settings.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade and tips are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the app never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro has been bought on your account. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications, vibration and alarms: to tell you when a rest between attempts is over.
  • Camera / photos: only when you choose to attach a photo to a climb. The photo is copied into the app and stays on your phone.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
